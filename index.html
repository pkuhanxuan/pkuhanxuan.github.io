About

I am an incoming M.S. student at the School of Advanced Manufacturing and Robotics, Peking University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Zhibo Pang in the Industrial Embodied Intelligence Lab. I received my B.Eng. from the College of Engineering, Peking University in 2025.

My research focuses on dual-arm robot manipulation and foundation models for embodied intelligence. I am broadly interested in building robots that can learn general, dexterous manipulation skills from large-scale data.

Research Interests

  • Dual-arm / bimanual robot manipulation
  • Foundation models for robotics and embodied AI
  • Imitation learning and large-scale robot data
