About关于我

I am a master's student in Mechanical Engineering at the School of Mechanics and Engineering Science, Peking University (September 2025–expected July 2028), where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Zhibo Pang in the PKU PI-Lab. I received my B.Sc. in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics from the College of Engineering, Peking University (September 2021–June 2025).

My research focuses on dual-arm robot manipulation and foundation models for embodied intelligence. I am broadly interested in building robots that can learn general, dexterous manipulation skills from large-scale data.

我是北京大学力学与工程科学学院机械工程专业硕士研究生（2025 年 9 月入学，预计 2028 年 7 月毕业），师从庞智博教授，在物理智能实验室（PKU PI-Lab）从事研究。2021 年 9 月至 2025 年 6 月就读于北京大学工学院理论与应用力学专业，获理学学士学位。

我的研究方向是双臂机器人操作与具身智能基础模型，关注如何让机器人从大规模数据中学到通用、灵巧的操作技能。
