About关于我

I am an M.S. student at the School of Advanced Manufacturing and Robotics, Peking University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Zhibo Pang in the PKU PI-Lab. I received my B.Eng. from the College of Engineering, Peking University in 2025.

My research focuses on dual-arm robot manipulation and foundation models for embodied intelligence. I am broadly interested in building robots that can learn general, dexterous manipulation skills from large-scale data.

我是北京大学先进制造与机器人学院的硕士研究生，师从庞智博教授，在物理智能实验室（PKU PI-Lab）从事研究。2025 年本科毕业于北京大学工学院。

我的研究方向是双臂机器人操作与具身智能基础模型，关注如何让机器人从大规模数据中学到通用、灵巧的操作技能。
